ABOUT THE FEST

Experience Something Extraordinary

Innovista is a two-day college festival that brings together technology, creativity, culture, and entertainment under one roof.

Students from different colleges can participate in exciting competitions, showcase their skills, meet new people, and create unforgettable memories.

Why Participate?

  1. Showcase your talents
  2. Compete with talented students
  3. Win exciting prizes
  4. Build new connections
